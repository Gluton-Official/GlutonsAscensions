# Gluton's Ascensions

Adds 10 more ascensions.

11. Torn Rug - Merchants offer 1 less attack and skill, and no on-sale cards.
12. Out-of-Business - 1 less marked Merchant room in each act. (replaced with Unknown room)
13. Barren - Unknown rooms are less common. (replaced with Enemy combat)
14. Empty Flasks - Enemy combats no longer have a chance to drop potions.
15. Short Supply - Ancients only offer 2 relics.
16. Slim Pickings - Elite combat card rewards have 1 less card.
17. Plundered - No marked Treasure Rooms. (replaced with Unknown room)
18. Cold Comfort - Ancients heal only 20% of missing health.
19. Unprepared - Draw 1 less card at the start of each combat.
20. Locked-In - Starting Strikes and Defends gain Eternal.
